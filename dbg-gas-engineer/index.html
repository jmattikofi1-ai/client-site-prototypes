Safety first

Smell gas? Act straight away.

  • Call the National Gas Emergency Service right away: it's free, 24 hours a day.
  • Open doors and windows to let fresh air in.
  • Don't use light switches, naked flames or electrical appliances.
  • If it's safe to do so, turn off the gas at the meter.
