What we do

Every surface,
restored.

From weekly window rounds to one-off deep cleans, we tailor the service to your property, and quote you fairly.

01

Window cleaning

Residential and commercial window cleaning to a consistently high standard, inside and out, including frames, doors and sills.

02

Conservatory cleaning

High reach pure water systems and safe professional products for roofs, frames and glass.

03

Gutter cleaning

An annual clean helps you avoid expensive repairs. We'll advise what your roof and location need.

04

Fascias, soffits & cladding

Tired, dirty PVC returned to as-new condition, with the life and beauty back in your property.

05

Solar panel cleaning

The more dirt, the less electricity your panels generate. Keep them performing at their best.

06

Pressure washing

Paths, patios and driveways washed back to life. Once washed, we can sweep kiln-dried sand back into the gaps in block paving to help protect it.
