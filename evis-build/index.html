01 The Evis Build approach

Design → Improve → Build.

Three services across the entire cycle of a project — from concept design, detailed design and planning applications, to cost control, construction and final fit-out. Use one, or all three.

1 Design

Interior architecture, done properly.

Our expertise in interior architecture allows us to design your new space to suit you and your budget. Our input can be as little as selecting the finishes and fixtures for a new room, or as extensive as remodelling an entire floor plan and commissioning bespoke furniture.

  • Fixed fee for design and space planning — never charged by the hour
  • Planning applications and building regulation approvals
  • Party wall agreements and new home warranty applications
  • Bathrooms, kitchens, open-plan living, extensions, lofts and garages

2 Improve

Every home improvement, covered.

Whether you’re wanting to improve or extend your home, we have you covered — from painting and decorating, installing a new kitchen or bathroom, building an extension or loft conversion, to remodelling your entire floor area.

  • Extensions, conversions and new spaces
  • Internal works, external works and services
  • Hard landscaping — driveways, patios, decking and fencing
  • A construction programme you can plan around

03 How we price

A quote is a quote. Not an estimate.

We never start work on an estimate and then ask for more money later. Our prices are fixed — and you will never be quoted any different to our previous clients for like-for-like projects.

— Evis Build, on what makes us different

We charge you what the project costs us and then add a fixed profit on top. We compete on the quality of our service, workmanship and value for money rather than on price alone — we can’t promise to be the cheapest, but we can promise our quote will be the fairest and most realistic to complete your project on time, to your expectations, and without asking for a penny more once we start.

Most projects

Flat Quote

A free, no-obligation fixed quote provided after first contact. Provided for all design work and most building work. Valid for a year.

Bespoke projects

Select Quote

For bespoke work we price each element separately. It takes time to produce, so there’s an upfront fee — refunded if you accept the quote and instruct us as your builders. Valid for three months.

From first call to final payment

  1. Free initial meetingTalk through your project at a free home visit.
  2. Clear fixed quoteShows precisely what work we’ll undertake for the price.
  3. Contract signedBefore any work starts, with an agreed payment schedule.
  4. Programme providedAn easy-to-understand construction programme, so you know what’s happening each day.
  5. Retention held backYou retain part of every invoice, paid only once we’ve finished and left site.

04 Who we are

Doing things differently from your ordinary design and build company.

Evis Build is a residential design, home improvement and new build company. We work hard to be the most professional, trustworthy and efficient design and build company serving UK homeowners — meeting your expectations with the creativity of our space designs and the quality of our building work.

We’ve taken modern ideas and processes used in the commercial world for years, tweaked them to suit private residential projects, and applied them to every job we work on.

  • Thoughtful designAs creative and bold as you’d like, or as reserved as necessary — with the long-term practical elements and your budget in mind.
  • Materials we’ve researchedWe research the cost-versus-quality balance of every material and fixture we specify, so you get exceptional value without inferior products.
  • “Tidy site, tidy mind”We cover and protect furnishings and floors, tidy up at the end of each working day and store deliveries so they inconvenience you and your neighbours least.

Founder & owner

Thomas Evison

Tom’s enthusiasm for design and build began young, as an apprentice on construction projects with a variety of trades. He later gained a degree in Construction and Cost Management and worked on high-profile commercial projects — where he saw how private residential construction could be improved.

Evis Build was born: a client-focused, professional and competitive design and build company for homeowners and self-builders.
