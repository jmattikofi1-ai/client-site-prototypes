What we do

Pick a side. Everything is covered.

  • House extensions

    Extra bedroom, bathroom, storage, office or kitchen space, single or double.

    An extra bedroom, bathroom or office. We can use our architect or work from your plans.

  • Bathroom refurbishments

    Supply and fit, or fit and tile only if you have already bought your suite.

  • Kitchen refurbishments

    From design stage to completion, including electrical and gas work with certificates.

  • Property renovations

    From a simple gutter clean through to a full re-roof.

    Time-served plasterers: re-skims, patch-ups and full houses, plus damp proofing.

    Boilers, central heating and gas safety reports, with paperwork on completion.

  • Architectural service

    Our architects turn your ideas into reality, with sound knowledge of what your local authority will allow.

  • Structural alterations

    Load-bearing wall removal, new openings and blocking up existing ones.

  • Groundwork & drainage

    Footings, drainage, new mains water supplies and concrete work, by hand dig or by digger.
