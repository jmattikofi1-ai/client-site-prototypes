MOT questions

        
Quick answers about the MOT

        
General guidance only – if you’re unsure about your vehicle, just give us a call.

      

      

        

          

          

            
In the UK, a car needs an MOT test every year once it is 3 years old. The period varies for some other vehicle types. It’s also worth knowing you can usually have the test up to a month (minus a day) before your current MOT runs out and keep the same renewal date – ask us if you’re not sure.

          

        

        

          

          

            
The test checks that your vehicle meets minimum road safety and environmental standards – including steering, suspension, brakes, lights, wipers and washers, tyres and wheels, seatbelts, exhaust emissions and the condition of the body and structure. See the full inspection list above; it isn’t exhaustive.

          

        

        

          

          

            
For a typical car the test itself usually takes under an hour, but it depends on the vehicle and how busy the station is. Call us on 01908 210055 when you book and we’ll tell you what to expect.

          

        

        

          

          

            
Faults are recorded as dangerous, major, minor or advisory. A vehicle with a dangerous or major fault fails, and a dangerous fault means it shouldn’t be driven until repaired.

            
We can carry out the repairs needed to pass – even if we didn’t do the test – and we’ll quote first and won’t carry out extra work without your authority. Ask us about retest arrangements when you book.

          

        

        

          

          

            
No. The MOT is a snapshot of your vehicle’s condition on the day and no guarantee you won’t have problems later. A regular service is a good idea alongside the annual MOT – and we can service your car without invalidating its warranty.

          

        

        

          

          

            
We test Class IV vehicles (cars and small vans) and Class VII MOTs. If you’re unsure which class your vehicle falls into, call us on 01908 210055.
