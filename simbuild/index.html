Power flushing

Power flushing cleans your central heating. A pump is connected to your pipework and water is driven through the system at high velocity, dislodging sludge and debris and carrying it out — so your heating can give out more heat and save you money on bills.

  • Our engineers are fully trained by Kamco to BS7593
  • Official chemicals and inhibitor used
  • A properly done power flush takes at least 6 hours, depending on system size and condition
  • Recommended just before fitting a new boiler

Power flushing

From sludge to clean — so your radiators heat evenly again

Corrosion deposits build up inside heating systems. Modern, compact boilers are more sensitive to debris in the water — it collects in the heat exchanger, causing noisy running, reduced efficiency and, in extreme cases, boiler failure.

  • Radiators cold at the bottom
  • Banging noises from the boiler
  • Noisy pipes
  • Boiler cutting out

A pump is connected to your pipework and water is driven through at high velocity and low pressure, forcing sludge out of the system. We use official Kamco chemicals and inhibitor, and our engineers are Kamco-trained to BS7593. Allow at least 6 hours.
